What we are called in for

Four kinds of problem, one way of working.

Most of our work arrives the same way: something measurable has gone wrong, the internal team has exhausted the obvious causes, and a customer or a launch date is now at risk.

01

  • Analytical campaign design

Method

Evidence first. Opinion last.

Anyone can produce a plausible story about why a material failed. The expensive mistake is acting on the wrong one.

We work the way a good laboratory works: state the candidate mechanisms explicitly, design the smallest set of measurements that can discriminate between them, and let the data eliminate the losers. You see the reasoning, not just the conclusion — which means your team can defend it to a customer, a regulator or a court without us in the room.

  • Every claim traced to a specific measurement, instrument and date
  • Alternative explanations documented and explicitly ruled out
  • Raw data handed over with the report — it is yours, not ours

Engagement

How an engagement actually runs.

Four stages, each with a written output you can act on. If stage one shows the problem is not ours to solve, we tell you and stop there.

Stage 01

Technical intake

A 60-minute call with the engineers who will do the work. You bring the symptoms, the history and whatever data exists. We leave with a candidate mechanism list.

Stage 02

Scoped proposal

A written plan: the hypotheses to be tested, the analytical methods, the laboratories, the timeline and a fixed fee. Nothing starts until you sign it.

Stage 03

Investigation

Sample preparation, measurement, modelling. Weekly written updates — including the results that do not support the leading hypothesis.

Stage 04

Report & handover

Mechanism, evidence, recommended corrective action and the residual risk we could not close. Raw data included. A debrief with your team, not a slide deck.
